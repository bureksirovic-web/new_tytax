**Status (G1 Wave 2 merged into v2-g3): done.** `snapToBells` is deleted; `availableKg` goes straight to G1's `prefillFromHistory`. Without bells a kettlebell now holds (G1 `nextKg`), asserted in `hardening.test.ts`.
